Frequently Asked Questions

What does HM UNIQUE Technology Enterprises supply?

HM UNIQUE lists five product categories: Electricals, Electronics Components, Hardware, Industrial & Commercial Equipments and Industrial Safety & Packing Materials. Each category page shows the product groups currently listed.

How do I request a quotation?

Select Request a Quote on any page, or contact the team at business@hmunique.com or +91 97101 81944. Please mention the product name or category, the quantity if you know it, and your city so the team can understand your requirement.

What should I include in an enquiry?

The product category, the product name if you know it, the quantity if you know it, your city and state, and a phone number and email address so the team can reach you.

Does this website show prices, stock or delivery times?

No. Prices, stock and delivery details are not listed on this website. Contact our team with your requirement to ask about a quotation and availability.

Where can I find the address and opening hours?

The office address, map and opening hours are on the Contact page. HM UNIQUE Technology Enterprises is located in George Town, Chennai.
